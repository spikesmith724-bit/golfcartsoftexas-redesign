Golf Carts in Dickinson, TX

A short run from FM 517 and Hwy 3 to our lot in Bacliff.

Dickinson does not advertise itself as a golf cart town, and that is part of why carts work so well here. The streets around Hwy 3 and FM 517 are mostly quiet. Dickinson Bayou cuts through the middle of town. Paul Hopkins Park and Nichols Park fill up on weekends, and a lot of the housing is in established neighborhoods rather than brand-new gated villages. People want a cart they can take down to a neighbor's house, over to the park, or along a bayou-side street without making a production of it.

Golf Carts of Texas is about six miles and ten to fifteen minutes from Dickinson City Hall. The main shop is at 3615 Hwy 146 in Bacliff. From most of Dickinson the easiest path is FM 517 east toward the bay, or a quick jog over to 146. Call 281-559-3171 for the office or 832-814-0452 for the shop cell. We can tell you what is on the lot before you drive over, which matters if you are coming on a weekday between school drop-off and the afternoon freight traffic on I-45.

We see a different mix of requests from Dickinson than we do from the bigger master-planned communities to the north and west. There are still customers who want a six-seater for family cookouts, but just as many ask for a solid used Club Car or E-Z-GO, a simple new four-seater, or a modest lift so the cart clears the uneven spots on an older street. Downtown Dickinson is small and practical. A cart here is often a second vehicle for the subdivision, not a showpiece for a resort gate.

That does not mean we only sell basic units. If you want a Tomberlin, a Solana, or a lifted Bintelli, we will build it the same way we would for anyone else on the Gulf Coast. Custom paint and sound systems are done in-house. What we will not do is talk you into a pile of accessories you will never use on a short trip to the bayou. If a used cart with fresh batteries is the honest answer, we will say so.

Our Galveston location at 3538 Broadway is about twenty-three miles and half an hour from Dickinson, so it is rarely the first stop unless you are already headed to the island. Service work, parts, and most deliveries run out of Bacliff. If your cart already lives off FM 517, Hwy 3, or one of the newer pockets toward League City, you are in our everyday service area, not on the edge of it.

Street-legal carts in Dickinson

Using a cart inside a private neighborhood is usually a question for the HOA or the property owner. Taking one onto public streets in Dickinson generally means a street-legal low-speed vehicle. In Texas, LSVs are typically limited to roads posted 35 mph or less and must be properly equipped for on-road use. Dickinson may have additional local rules, and those are not something we will guess at. Confirm the current requirements with the City of Dickinson, and we will help you choose a cart that can be set up for that kind of use if you need it.

Dickinson golf cart questions

How do I get to the shop from Dickinson?

Plan on about 6 miles and 10 to 15 minutes to 3615 Hwy 146 in Bacliff. FM 517 east toward the bay is the route most people use.

Do you have used golf carts in a Dickinson-friendly price range?

Inventory changes constantly, and we regularly have used and refurbished carts on the lot. Call 281-559-3171 or check the current inventory page for what is in stock this week.

Can you pick up a cart for service from a Dickinson address?

Often yes. Call the shop cell at 832-814-0452 and we will tell you whether a pickup is practical for your street and the work you need.

Will a lifted cart be too much for older Dickinson streets?

Not always, but it is a fair question. If the streets near you are uneven or narrow, we may recommend a milder lift or a lower cart. Come drive a few options on our lot and decide from there.
