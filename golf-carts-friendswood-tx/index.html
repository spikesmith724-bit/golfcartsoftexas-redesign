Golf Carts in Friendswood, TX

Tree-lined streets, West Ranch, and a 25-minute drive to the shop.

Friendswood still feels like a town, even as Houston has grown around it. The older streets are shaded and quiet. Downtown along Friendswood Drive and FM 518 has a small-town center rather than a giant retail strip. Then, on the newer side of town, West Ranch and similar developments added the kind of wide interior roads where a golf cart is no longer unusual. That mix is exactly why Friendswood customers show up at Golf Carts of Texas with two very different shopping lists: a modest cart that looks at home under the trees, or a larger, more finished cart for a master-planned neighborhood.

The drive is straightforward. Our main shop is at 3615 Hwy 146 in Bacliff, about fifteen miles and twenty-five minutes from Friendswood City Hall. Most people come across on FM 518 toward League City and then over to 146, or they drop south and work their way to the bay. Call the office at 281-559-3171 or the shop cell at 832-814-0452 so we can have a cart ready to look at. The Galveston location at 3538 Broadway is about thirty-four miles and forty-five minutes away, which makes it a backup more than a first stop unless you are already going to the island.

Friendswood is not on the water, and that changes the sale a little. Nobody is asking us for a cart to run to a boardwalk. They are asking for something that will get a family to Stevenson Park, to a friend's house on the other side of West Ranch, or down to the high school area without starting a full-size vehicle. Schools, youth sports, and neighborhood social life do more of the work here than beaches or marinas. We pay attention to that when we talk about seats, storage, and whether a lift is actually useful on smooth interior streets.

We sell new Tomberlin, Star EV, NIU EV, Solana, Bintelli, ActivEV, and Aries EV carts, and we keep used and refurbished carts on the lot as they come in. Custom paint and accessories are available if you want the cart to feel finished, but we will not treat a Friendswood purchase like a theme-park build unless that is what you asked for. A lot of our neighbors in this direction want something reliable, quiet, and easy to charge at home.

If your HOA has a written rule about carts, bring it. West Ranch and some of the older associations do not always treat carts the same way. We cannot negotiate with an HOA for you, but we can avoid selling you a lighting package or a lift that is going to be a fight the first week you park in the driveway.

Street-legal carts in Friendswood

Most Friendswood riding happens inside neighborhoods, but some owners want the option to use certain public streets. In Texas, a street-legal low-speed vehicle is generally limited to roads posted 35 mph or less and must be properly equipped for on-road use. Friendswood may have its own local rules as well. We will not guess at a city ordinance. Check with the City of Friendswood, then we can talk about whether an LSV setup is the right purchase for the way you actually get around town.

Friendswood golf cart questions

How long does it take to get to the shop from Friendswood?

About 15 miles and 25 minutes to our Bacliff location at 3615 Hwy 146. The Galveston shop is about 45 minutes, so Bacliff is the usual stop.

Do West Ranch and older Friendswood streets need different carts?

Sometimes. Wider master-planned streets can take a larger or lifted cart more comfortably. Older, tighter streets may be happier with a lower four-seater. Tell us where you live and we will narrow it down.

Can I trade in a cart I already have?

Often yes. Bring the cart to Bacliff or send us photos and the details, and we will tell you if a trade makes sense on a new or used unit.

Do you finance carts for Friendswood buyers?

Yes, through Octane. Apply online and then call 281-559-3171 so we can line up the cart and any work you want done.
