Golf Carts in Clear Lake, TX

NASA, the lake, and Houston Bay Area neighborhoods, about 15 to 20 minutes away.

Clear Lake is one of those Houston names that means more than a single set of city limits. People say Clear Lake when they mean Clear Lake City inside Houston, the neighborhoods around NASA Parkway and El Camino Real, the streets that back up to the lake itself, and the small cities that share the water: Nassau Bay, El Lago, Taylor Lake Village, and the edge of Seabrook. Golf Carts of Texas has been selling into that whole Bay Area for years, and the shop is closer than a lot of Houston residents expect. From Clear Lake City to 3615 Hwy 146 in Bacliff is about ten miles and fifteen to twenty minutes, mostly a straight shot toward the bay.

Call the office at 281-559-3171 or the shop cell at 832-814-0452. We are two miles south of Kemah, which puts us on the same mental map as a weekend trip to the water. If you are already heading to Galveston, our second location at 3538 Broadway is about thirty-two miles and forty minutes from Clear Lake. For a first look at a cart, a battery question, or a custom build conversation, Bacliff is the stop that makes sense.

The character of this area is a blend of NASA, the lake, and suburban Houston. Bay Oaks, Bay Forest, Pinebrook, Brook Forest, and the older Clear Lake City sections each have a slightly different street pattern, but they share a habit of short trips: to a neighbor's house, to a community pool, to Clear Lake Park, or over toward the marinas. Some households also keep a boat, which is why we talk about garage space, charging, and whether a cart will live next to a trailer. This is not a Gulf beach town, and it is not a brand-new master-planned suburb in the Pearland mold. It is a settled Bay Area community with a lot of people who already know what a cart is for.

We sell new carts from Tomberlin, Star EV, NIU EV, Solana, Bintelli, ActivEV, and Aries EV, and we take used Club Car and E-Z-GO carts in trade. Because so many Clear Lake customers already own something, we do a lot of service and upgrade work: new batteries, lifts, seats, lighting, and the occasional full custom paint job. If your current cart has become a weekend headache, it is worth asking whether a repair or a replacement is the better spend before you buy.

If you live in a section that sits inside the City of Houston, or in one of the smaller lakeside cities next door, bring that detail with you. The lake is shared. The local rules are not always the same. We will help you choose the cart; you will want the right city office to confirm where it can go on public streets.

Street-legal carts in Clear Lake

Clear Lake riders may be dealing with the City of Houston, with a smaller neighboring city, or with an HOA, depending on the exact street. For public roads, a street-legal low-speed vehicle in Texas is generally limited to roads posted 35 mph or less and must be properly equipped for on-road use. We will not invent a Houston ordinance or a Nassau Bay rule for you. Check with the city that actually maintains your street, then we can help you decide whether an LSV is worth adding to the purchase.

Clear Lake golf cart questions

How far is the shop from Clear Lake City?

About 10 miles and 15 to 20 minutes to 3615 Hwy 146 in Bacliff. We are just south of Kemah on the same bay-area route.

Do you serve Nassau Bay, El Lago, and Seabrook too?

Yes. Those lakeside cities are part of the same Clear Lake / Bay Area we already serve. Travel times are in the same range as Clear Lake City, and sometimes shorter.

Can I get a street-legal cart for Houston streets in Clear Lake?

Possibly, if it is set up as a low-speed vehicle and you stay on roads that are legal for LSVs. Confirm current rules with the City of Houston or with the small city you actually live in. We will help with the cart and the equipment.

Do you work on carts already in Bay Oaks or Brook Forest?

We service all makes and models at the Bacliff shop. Call 832-814-0452 and we can talk about drop-off or a possible pickup.
