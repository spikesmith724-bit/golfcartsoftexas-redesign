Golf Carts in Pearland, TX

A worthwhile drive from Shadow Creek Ranch and the rest of Pearland.

Pearland is the farthest of the eight communities we write about here, and it is still an easy, familiar drive for us. The reason is not geography alone. It is the size of the neighborhoods. Shadow Creek Ranch is a small city of its own, with parks, lakes, and enough interior road that a golf cart is a genuine household vehicle, not a novelty. Silverlake, Southern Trails, Lakes of Savannah, and the pockets around Pearland Town Center have the same habit: kids, dogs, evening loops, and weekend visits that never need to get on 288. When Pearland customers call Golf Carts of Texas, they are usually shopping for a cart that will live inside one of those communities every day.

The main shop is at 3615 Hwy 146 in Bacliff, about twenty miles and thirty-five minutes from Pearland City Hall in ordinary traffic. Your exact time depends on whether you come across FM 518, drop down from the Beltway, or take a 288 path and cut east. Call 281-559-3171 or 832-814-0452 before you leave so you are not driving thirty-five minutes to look at an empty space on the lot. Our Galveston shop at 3538 Broadway is about forty miles and fifty-five minutes from Pearland, which makes it the long way unless you are combining the trip with a day on the island.

What Pearland buyers tend to want is space. Six-seaters, forward-facing family layouts, and carts with enough cargo room for a ball game at the Pearland Sports Complex or a picnic at a Shadow Creek park show up on our order list over and over. Lifted carts are popular too, but they are a style choice as much as a terrain choice; the streets inside these communities are generally smooth. We will say so, and then we will still sell you a lifted cart if that is the look you want.

We are a family-owned authorized Tomberlin dealer, and we also carry Star EV, NIU EV, Solana, Bintelli, ActivEV, and Aries EV. Used Club Car and E-Z-GO carts come through as trade-ins. Custom paint, lighting, and audio are done at the Bacliff shop. If you already own a cart in Shadow Creek or Silverlake and it has started to lose range in the summer, we can look at batteries and charging before you spend the money on a whole new vehicle.

Bring HOA guidelines if your community has them in writing. Large Pearland associations sometimes have opinions about street-legal equipment, speed, or appearance. We cannot overrule an HOA, but we can help you avoid a purchase that is going to be a problem the first time you roll past the amenity center.

Street-legal carts in Pearland

Inside a gated or private community, the association's rules usually matter more than a city street map. If you want to use public streets in Pearland, you are generally looking at a street-legal low-speed vehicle. In Texas, LSVs are typically limited to roads posted 35 mph or less and must be properly equipped for on-road use. Pearland can also set its own local rules. We will not invent one for you. Confirm the current requirements with the City of Pearland, then we can help you buy or equip a cart with that use in mind.

Pearland golf cart questions

Is Pearland too far from the shop?

Most customers make the trip in about 35 minutes to our Bacliff location, roughly 20 miles. Call first so we can confirm the cart you want is on the lot.

What size cart do Shadow Creek Ranch families usually buy?

Six-seaters are the most common request we hear from large Pearland communities, but it depends on the household. Come sit in a four-seater and a six-seater before you decide.

Can you deliver a cart to Pearland?

Ask when you call. We can often arrange delivery or a pickup of a trade-in, depending on the address and the cart.
