Custom Builds

Illustrations of a monster-cart build, plus the shop comic.

This is the shop-mascot build series: a custom cart going from jack stands and a four-post lift, through welding, an engine hoist, paint, and out the door for a launch and a burnout in the lot. These are illustrations, not photos of a customer's cart. Real builds from the shop are in the photo gallery and on the custom golf carts page.

Every still on this page is an artist's rendering created for Golf Carts of Texas. It is not a photograph of a finished customer vehicle.

The Trade-In

From a tired pink cart to a monster cart

The short version of the shop comic: a worn-out lace-canopy cart rolls onto the lot. One handshake later, the mascot is in the bay with a welder, a hoist, and a bad attitude toward stock motors. The old cart comes apart. A new engine goes in. What rolls out is a flame-job monster cart, ready to leave rubber in the parking lot.

It's a comic, not a customer album — but the shop really does take trade-ins and build customs that turn heads. Bring us the cart you have, or just an idea, and we will tell you what we can do.
