Golf Carts in Galveston, TX

A Broadway shop on the island, plus full service up in Bacliff.

On Galveston Island a golf cart is part of the scenery, as familiar as a beach umbrella or a fishing rod leaned against a porch. You see them near the Strand, along the east-end historic streets, in west-end subdivisions, and in second-home communities where the whole point of the weekend is to leave the truck parked. Golf Carts of Texas keeps a location at 3538 Broadway, right on the island's main spine, so you do not have to get back on the causeway just to look at a cart, ask about parts, or start a conversation about a custom build.

The Broadway shop is about a mile, or a few minutes, from the Strand and from mid-island neighborhoods around 35th Street. Call 409-256-7717 to make sure someone can meet you. Our main location and service department remain in Bacliff, at 3615 Hwy 146, about twenty-five miles and thirty-five minutes from the island once you cross the causeway. Heavier service work, many custom builds, and a deeper stock of carts still run through Bacliff. A lot of island customers start on Broadway and finish a build or a repair up in Bacliff, which is a straightforward drive when you are ready.

Galveston is not one neighborhood. The east end and the Strand are tight, historic, and busy. The Seawall is a long public corridor with its own habits and rules. Out west, places like Pirates Beach, Spanish Grant, Isla del Sol, and the pockets toward Jamaica Beach feel more like residential enclaves, where a cart is how you get to a friend's house or a pocket park without getting back on Stewart Road. Beachtown, at the far eastern edge, is its own cart-friendly world. We do not assume that a west-end family and an east-end condo owner want the same vehicle, because they almost never do.

Salt, sun, and short trips are hard on batteries and metal, so we talk honestly about maintenance. A pretty cart that cannot hold a charge after a summer on the island is not a bargain. We sell new Tomberlin, Star EV, NIU EV, Solana, Bintelli, ActivEV, and Aries EV carts, and we take in used trade-ins that we inspect before they go back out. Street-legal equipment is a frequent request here, more so than in most mainland suburbs, because so many island errands involve a public street rather than a private loop.

If you are buying a cart for a vacation house, bring the house rules and a sense of where you actually ride. We would rather set you up once than have you discover, after the ferry or the causeway trip home, that the cart does not fit the garage or the way you use the island.

Street-legal carts in Galveston

Galveston is a city of public streets, a seawall, and a lot of visitor traffic, so street-legal questions come up constantly. In Texas, a low-speed vehicle is generally limited to roads posted 35 mph or less and must be properly equipped and titled for on-road use. The City of Galveston may also have its own rules about where those vehicles can operate. We will not invent an ordinance or promise that a cart is legal on a particular stretch of the island. Check with the city, then let us help you with an LSV setup that matches what you were told.

Galveston golf cart questions

Where is the Galveston shop?

3538 Broadway, Galveston, TX 77550. Call 409-256-7717. From the Strand or mid-island it is typically about a mile.

Do I have to go to Bacliff for service?

Start with the Broadway shop. The full service department is in Bacliff, about 25 miles and 35 minutes from the island, and that is where heavier repairs and many custom builds are finished.

Can I drive a golf cart on the Seawall?

Do not take our word as a traffic rule. Seawall and other public roads are subject to Texas LSV limits and to whatever the City of Galveston currently allows. Ask the city, then talk to us about a street-legal cart if you need one.
