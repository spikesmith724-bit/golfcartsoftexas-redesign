Golf Carts in Kemah, TX

Two miles south of the Boardwalk, on the same stretch of Hwy 146.

Kemah is the first town most people name when they think of the bay, and it is also the closest town to our front door. Golf Carts of Texas sits at 3615 Hwy 146 in Bacliff, about two miles and five minutes south of Kemah City Hall and the Boardwalk. If you have ever driven 146 on a Saturday, you already know the route: past the marina, past the restaurants, and a couple of lights later you are in our lot. Call the office at 281-559-3171 or the shop cell at 832-814-0452 before you come if you want us to pull a particular cart around.

Life in Kemah is tied to the water in a way that most Houston suburbs are not. People keep boats in the Kemah Boardwalk Marina, walk over to the channel, and hop between 2nd Street, the Boardwalk, and the older bayfront streets that sit just off the highway. A cart is how a lot of locals move through that small, busy core without hunting for a parking space every time they want dinner or need to check a slip. We also see carts coming over from Clear Lake Shores and Seabrook, which share the same stretch of water and the same habit of running short errands on something smaller than a truck.

This is not a master-planned community in the League City sense. Kemah is compact. Lots are tighter, weekend traffic is heavier, and a flashy lifted cart that looks at home on a wide interior boulevard can feel oversized on a narrow bay street. Plenty of customers still want a lifted cart with serious wheels, especially if they also drive it in a nearby neighborhood or down to the island. Others ask for a lower, more maneuverable four-seater they can park beside a boat trailer. We sell both, and we will tell you honestly if a build is going to be a pain in a tight garage.

Because we are so close, Kemah customers often treat the shop the way they would a neighborhood garage. They stop in to look at used trade-ins, to add a windshield before summer, or to have us look at a battery pack that has started to fade in the heat and salt air. We are an authorized Tomberlin dealer, and we also carry Star EV, NIU EV, Solana, Bintelli, ActivEV, and Aries EV. Custom paint, stereo work, and lift kits are done in Bacliff, not sent across town.

If you are heading to the island instead, our second location is at 3538 Broadway in Galveston, about twenty-six miles and thirty-five minutes from Kemah. Most people who live here still come to the Bacliff shop. It is the one you can reach before a Boardwalk parking lot even starts to fill up.

Street-legal carts in Kemah

A cart that stays on private property or inside a marina compound is one thing. Taking it onto public streets in Kemah is another. Street-legal low-speed vehicles in Texas are generally limited to roads posted 35 mph or less, and they must be set up for on-road use. Kemah can add its own requirements, and we will not invent a local rule for you. Ask the City of Kemah about where LSVs may be operated, then we can help with the equipment and the purchase.

Kemah golf cart questions

Where is the shop from the Kemah Boardwalk?

Head south on Hwy 146 about 2 miles, or roughly 5 minutes. We are at 3615 Hwy 146 in Bacliff, just south of Kemah.

Can I take a golf cart to the Boardwalk?

That depends on where you are riding and whether the cart is set up as a street-legal LSV. Check with the City of Kemah for current local rules. We can help with the vehicle side, not with a legal opinion.

Do you work on carts that sit at the marina?

Yes. Salt air and short trips are hard on batteries, brakes, and wiring. Bring the cart to the Bacliff shop or call 832-814-0452 about pickup.

Is the Galveston location closer if I am already on 146?

No. From Kemah, Bacliff is a few minutes south. The Galveston shop is about 35 minutes farther down, after you reach the island.
