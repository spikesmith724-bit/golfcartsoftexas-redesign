Golf cart wheel sizes explained

Golf cart wheels come in four standard diameters: 8", 10", 12" and 14". The second number is the wheel's depth, so a 10×7 wheel measures 10" across and 7" deep.

Your look, your style, starting with a durable, safe tire. Every tire we carry meets stringent standards for stability and durability, with premium compounds for longer tread life.

Call or stop by to see our wheel and tire selection. We also carry color wheel inserts to dress up your wheels.
