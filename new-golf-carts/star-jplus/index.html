Star EV

J Plus

  • 2, 4, or 6 passengers
  • Up to 25 mph
  • Street-legal LSV

The STAR EV J-Plus is offered in multiple 2-, 4-, and 6-passenger configurations, including lifted models. Specs below are shown for each official configuration.

Golf Carts of Texas also installs custom accessories — lift kits, wheels, seats, lighting, sound and more. Browse our Accessories pages to see what we can add.

Specifications

Street-legal / LSVStreet-legal LSV; 100% compliant with federal LSV safety standards (FMVSS No. 500), including required lighting, mirrors, seat belts, and a 25 mph top speed.
Warranty4-year limited warranty on major components such as the frame and suspension; 2-year bumper-to-bumper warranty (excluding typical wear items such as tires and brake pads); STAR lithium batteries (LiFePO₄) are covered under a separate warranty, typically offering up to 6.5 years of coverage depending on usage, effective from the date of purchase from an authorized STAR EV dealer.
Colors6 colors, including sky blue, pepper gray, black, and white
