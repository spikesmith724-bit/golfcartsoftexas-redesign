Fleet & Government Sales

Carts, upfitting and service for agencies, campuses and facilities.

For public agencies, schools & facilities

Golf carts and low-speed vehicles for fleets

Golf Carts of Texas supplies, customizes and services golf carts and utility vehicles for organizations that move people and equipment across campuses, parks, plants and properties. We sell new carts from Tomberlin, Star EV, NIU EV, Solana, Bintelli, ActivEV and Aries EV, and as an authorized Tomberlin dealer and service center we can outfit them for the job and keep the whole fleet running, regardless of make.

Who we serve

Cities & counties

Parks and recreation, public works, code enforcement, event and beach patrol.

Schools & universities

Campus transport, athletics, facilities and maintenance crews.

State & federal facilities

Hospitals and VA campuses, military installations, ports and agency sites.

Commercial & industrial

Plants, refineries, warehouses, marinas, RV parks, apartment and HOA communities.

What we provide

New fleet carts

New carts from Tomberlin, Star EV, NIU EV, Solana, Bintelli, ActivEV and Aries EV, in configurations to fit campuses, parks and facilities. Call for current models and availability.

Upfitting & branding

Agency or company colors and logos, lighting, lift kits, all-terrain tires, brush guards, windshields, enclosures, hitches and cargo storage.

Fleet maintenance & repair

Repair, rebuild or refurbish any golf cart or industrial vehicle from any manufacturer, gas or electric: scheduled service, diagnostics, electrical, tune-ups.

Batteries & charging

Authorized Trojan Battery dealer. Battery testing, replacement and charging-system service for electric fleets.

Pre-owned & refurbished units

Budget-friendly refurbished carts when a new unit isn't required.

Parts & accessories

Parts and accessories for Tomberlin, Club Car, E-Z-GO and Yamaha.

Bids, RFQs & quotes

We respond to quote requests and formal solicitations from city, county, state and federal buyers and from institutions. Send your specs, quantities, delivery location and due date and we'll respond with pricing and lead times.
