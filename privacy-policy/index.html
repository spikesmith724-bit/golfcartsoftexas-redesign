Privacy Policy

Updated November 2026

This policy explains how Golf Carts of Texas, 3615 Hwy 146, Bacliff, TX 77518 ("we", "us"), handles information when you visit golfcartsoftexas.com (the "Site").

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you call, email or use the contact form, we receive what you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address and message. The contact form on this Site opens your own email app; nothing is stored on the Site itself.

Usage data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. If we use analytics tools such as Google Analytics, they collect similar usage data to help us understand how the Site is used.

Cookies. The Site itself does not require cookies. Embedded content from third parties (YouTube, Vimeo, Google Maps) and our financing partner's website may set their own cookies under their own policies.

How we use information

  • To respond to your questions, quotes and service requests
  • To provide and complete purchases, financing referrals and service work you request
  • To send updates or offers related to products and services you've asked about, unless you opt out
  • To maintain, secure and improve the Site

When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with service providers who help us run our business (for example, website hosting or email), with a lender only if you choose to apply for financing, in connection with a merger or sale of the business, or when required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. We use reasonable measures to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other sites

The Site links to third-party sites such as our financing partner, Facebook and Instagram. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
