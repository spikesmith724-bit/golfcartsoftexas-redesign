Golf Cart Battery Maintenance

Eight tips to keep your batteries in top shape.

Battery maintenance made simple

Good battery maintenance gets the best performance out of your cart, whether it's a Tomberlin, Club Car, E-Z-GO or Yamaha. Here are our top eight tips.

We recommend Trojan golf cart batteries.

  1. Keep battery tops dry, clean and tight.

    Clean with a bristle brush and a baking soda and water solution. Wear eye protection and rubber gloves, and no jewelry. Anti-corrosion spray on the cables helps prevent corrosion and rust.

  2. Charge 8 to 10 hours with the proper charger.

    This assumes an 80% discharge. Best practice is to charge overnight after every use, even if you only drove for 5 minutes.

  3. Fill cells only after charging, and don't overfill.

    Use distilled water (not tap water) ¼" to ½" above the plates. At ¼" you'll check more often; at ½" you may see more acid on the battery tops. Either is acceptable.

  4. Use your batteries often.

    Do a refresh charge every 45 to 60 days, more often in hot climates. A charger with a storage mode will refresh automatically while plugged in if you use your cart seasonally.

  5. Don't overcharge.

    If you have an automatic charger, this is handled for you.

  6. Don't discharge beyond 80%.

    Aim to discharge between 50% and 80%, never until the batteries are completely dead.

  7. Check water levels regularly.

    Fill when needed. Not sure how? Come see us.

  8. Record each battery's voltage.

    Keep a record for future reference so you can spot a weak battery early.
