Golf Carts of Texas

Capabilities Statement · Golf Carts, Low-Speed Utility Vehicles, Upfitting & Fleet Service

Company overview

Golf Carts of Texas is a family-owned golf cart dealer, custom builder and service center in Bacliff, Texas, with a Galveston location, on the Houston–Galveston Gulf Coast. We sell new carts from Tomberlin, Star EV, NIU EV, Solana, Bintelli, ActivEV and Aries EV. We are an authorized Tomberlin dealer and service center and an authorized dealer for Sky Electric Vehicles and Trojan Batteries. We also sell custom and pre-owned carts and service all makes and models, gas and electric, for business, institutional and personal use.

Core competencies

  • New golf carts and personal transportation vehicles from Tomberlin, Star EV, NIU EV, Solana, Bintelli, ActivEV and Aries EV
  • Repair, rebuild and refurbishment of golf carts and industrial/utility vehicles from any manufacturer
  • Battery supply, testing and replacement (authorized Trojan dealer); charging-system diagnostics
  • Fleet upfitting: logos and custom paint, lighting, lift kits, tires and wheels, brush guards, windshields, enclosures, seating, hitches and storage
  • Pre-owned and refurbished carts; parts and accessories for Tomberlin, Club Car, E-Z-GO and Yamaha

Differentiators

  • Authorized manufacturer dealer and service center, with sales, upfitting and service under one roof
  • Bumper-to-bumper warranty on all new carts and custom builds
  • Multi-brand service capability: one vendor for mixed fleets
  • Local Gulf Coast presence with locations in Bacliff and Galveston

Past performance

Serving residential, commercial and community customers across Greater Houston, Clear Lake, Kemah, League City, Galveston and the Texas Gulf Coast. References available on request.
